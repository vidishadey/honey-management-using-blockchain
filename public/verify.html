🎓 Understanding This Verification: Three Critical Dimensions

As part of the academic Honey Chain project, it is essential for consumers, beekeepers, and examination evaluators to distinguish between the three distinct levels of verification provided:

1. The Application Record (What Was Entered)

The harvest location, dates, and yield parameters displayed above reflect the data officially input by the registered beekeeper.

2. Cryptographic Blockchain Integrity (Proof Against Alteration)

SHA-256 cryptographic hashes guarantee that since this batch was recorded into the ledger, no data has been modified, forged, or retroactively edited by any party.

3. Physical Honey Purity Limitation (The "Oracle Problem")

Academic Note: A blockchain ledger protects information against undetected digital alteration. However, software algorithms cannot independently prove that physical honey in the jar is pure without accredited laboratory pollen/C4-sugar analysis and tamper-evident physical seals.
